participant "Connector Module" as CM
participant "User Manager" as UM
participant "Data Access Module" as DAM

User -> UI: Confirm

UM -> DAM: deleteUser(userId)

DAM --> UM: success

UM --> CM: deletion success
CM --> UI: Redirect to login
